吉諾分帳所 Gino's Split Lab
更新日期：2026-10-29

吉諾分帳所是免費的記帳分帳工具：出去玩、一起住的共同花費記一筆，就算好誰該付誰。有 iPhone App 和網頁版 split.ginochang.com，兩邊用同一個帳號、看到同一本帳。沒有廣告，也不追蹤。

換新手機，怎麼登入？

  • 帳號有 Email：在新手機打開網頁版 split.ginochang.com，點「已有帳號？登入」，輸入 Email，再輸入寄給你的 6 位數驗證碼，不用密碼。帳號有密碼的話，在 iPhone App 或網頁版也可以用 Email 和密碼登入。
  • 帳號沒有 Email：在還登入著的手機或電腦打開「我的」（iPhone App 裡是「我的 › 裝置與在其他裝置登入」），點「在其他裝置登入」，會出現 8 碼的登入碼（10 分鐘內有效，只能用一次）。在新手機的 App 點「用其他裝置的登入碼」，輸入這組碼。
  • 舊裝置都不見了，帳號也沒有 Email：請群組的管理員在 App 或網頁版的群組設定裡打開你的名字，點「幫他重新登入」，再把產生的連結傳給你。用這個連結就能接回原本的名字，帳目都還在。用 iPhone App 的話，先在新手機輸入名字、點「開始使用」，再點「總覽」右上角的「＋」，選「用邀請連結加入群組」，貼上連結；直接點開連結會開到網頁版。每個群組要各做一次。

建議先在「我的」加上 Email，以後換手機最省事。

可以用 Apple 或 Google 登入嗎？

可以：網頁版，以及 1.1 版起的 iPhone App。在「開始使用」或「登入」的畫面，Email 欄位上面有「使用 Apple 登入」和「使用 Google 帳戶登入」，點了會到 Apple 或 Google 的畫面登入，再回到本服務。已經有帳號用同一個 Email 的話，會登入那個帳號，並把 Apple 或 Google 連到它；沒有的話，會建立新帳號，之後可以在「我的」改名字。Email 驗證碼和密碼也照樣能用。

用 Apple 登入時選了「隱藏我的電子郵件」的話，Apple 給本服務的是一個轉寄地址，找不到你原本的帳號，所以會建立一個新的。已經有帳號的話：先登出，用原本的方式登入，再到「我的 › 登入方式」連結 Apple；那個新帳號如果還沒有群組，就會刪除，Apple 改連到你原本的帳號。Google 帳戶的 Email 不一樣時，也可以這樣連結。從 Google 的頁面（以及網頁版從 Apple 的頁面）回來時，會先問你要不要連結那個帳戶，按「連結」才會連上。

在 LINE、Facebook、Instagram 或其他 App 裡面打開的網頁，不會出現這兩個按鈕（Google 不讓你在那裡登入，在那裡的登入之後也容易不見）；請照頁面上的提示，改用 Safari 或 Chrome 打開。

連結後，「我的」的登入方式會顯示 Apple 或 Google「已連結」。目前還不能自己取消連結，想取消的話，請寫信給我。本服務從 Apple 和 Google 拿到哪些資料，請看隱私權政策。

忘記密碼怎麼辦？

不用重設：在網頁版的登入頁輸入你的 Email，點「寄驗證碼」，再輸入寄給你的 6 位數驗證碼就能登入，不用密碼。有密碼的帳號也可以這樣登入：在密碼表單點「忘記密碼？用驗證碼登入」。登入後可以在「我的」設定新密碼，之後在 iPhone App 也用新密碼登入。

如果你還有登入著的裝置，也可以先用上面的登入碼登入新裝置，不用急著重設。

怎麼刪除帳號？

iPhone App：「我的 › 刪除帳號」。網頁版：「我的」頁面下方的「刪除帳號」。有密碼的帳號要先輸入密碼。

帳號會被刪除，所有裝置都會登出。群組裡的紀錄會留著（包含你附上的收據照片），你的名字會變成還沒人認領的成員，其他人的餘額才不會出錯。刪除後無法復原。詳細內容請看隱私權政策。

收據照片存在哪裡、誰看得到？

在網頁版新增或編輯費用時，點「附收據」拍一張或從相簿選一張，照片就會和這筆帳一起存起來；用「掃描」讀過的照片也會自動附上，儲存前按 × 可以移除。照片會先在你的裝置上縮小（最長邊最多 1600 像素），照片裡的拍攝地點等資料會一起拿掉，再存到 Cloudflare Workers KV。同一個群組的人都看得到，也都可以在這筆帳的明細裡移除它。掃描時送去辨識的那一份仍然用完就丟掉，由 Cloudflare Workers AI 辨識。

刪除這筆帳後，照片會再保留一天讓你可以復原，之後一到兩天內刪除；刪除群組後，一天內刪除。刪除帳號不會刪掉你上傳的收據照片，因為它們屬於群組的帳。

分不完的零頭怎麼算？

金額都用幣別的最小單位計算：新台幣和日圓算到 1 元，美元算到 1 分。例如 100 元三個人平分，會分成 34、33、33 元。多出來的 1 元給誰，是由這筆費用自己算出的固定順序決定，不是每次都給付錢的人：同一筆再存一次不會換人，很多筆下來，零頭會分散到不同人身上。

用外幣記的帳，會先把總額換成群組的幣別，再分給每個人。每一筆的分攤加起來永遠剛好等於總額，所以群組裡所有人的餘額加起來一定是 0。

還有問題？

寫信到 gino@ginochang.com。回報問題時，請附上你用的是 App 還是網頁版、App 的版本（在「我的」最下面），以及發生了什麼事。

Gino's Split Lab (吉諾分帳所)
Last updated: October 29, 2026

Gino's Split Lab is a free app for shared costs on trips and at home: log a cost once and it works out who owes whom. It comes as an iPhone app and as the web app at split.ginochang.com, which share one account and the same books. No ads, no tracking.

How do I sign in on a new phone?

  • Your account has an email: on the new phone, open the web app at split.ginochang.com, tap "Have an account? Sign in", enter your email, then the 6-digit code emailed to you. No password needed. If your account has a password, you can also sign in with your email and password in the iPhone app or on the web.
  • Your account has no email: on a phone or computer that is still signed in, open Account (in the iPhone app, Account › Devices and signing in elsewhere) and tap "Sign in on another device". An 8-character code appears (valid for 10 minutes, one use). In the app on the new phone, tap "Use a code from another device" and type it.
  • Your old devices are gone and the account has no email: ask an admin of your group to open the group's settings in the app or on the web, choose your name and tap "Help them sign in again", then send you the link. The link brings back your name with every entry. If you use the iPhone app, type a name on the new phone and tap "Get started", then tap + at the top right of Home, choose "Join with an invite link" and paste the link; opening the link directly takes you to the web app. Each group needs its own link.

Adding an email under Account makes the next new phone easy.

Can I sign in with Apple or Google?

Yes: on the web, and in the iPhone app from version 1.1. On the first screen and on Sign in, "Sign in with Apple" and "Sign in with Google" sit above the email field. They take you to Apple's or Google's screen to sign in, then back to the service. If an account already uses the same email, you sign in to it and Apple or Google is linked to it; if not, a new account is made, whose name you can change under Account. Email codes and passwords keep working too.

If you choose Hide My Email when you sign in with Apple, Apple gives the service a forwarding address, so your existing account can't be found and a new one is made. If you already have an account: sign out, sign in the way you used to, then link Apple under Account › Sign-in; if the new account has no groups yet, it is deleted and Apple moves to your account. A Google account with a different email can be linked the same way. Coming back from Google's pages (and on the web from Apple's), you are asked first whether to link that account; it is linked only when you tap Link.

Inside LINE, Facebook, Instagram or other apps' built-in browsers the two buttons are not shown (Google does not allow signing in there, and a sign-in made there is easily lost later); follow the note on the page to open it in Safari or Chrome instead.

Once linked, Account shows Apple or Google as "Linked" under sign-in. You can't unlink them yourself yet; email me to unlink one. For what the service gets from Apple and Google, see the privacy policy.

I forgot my password

No reset needed: on the web app's sign-in page, enter your email, tap "Send code", and enter the 6-digit code emailed to you. No password needed. Accounts with a password can sign in this way too: on the password form, tap "Forgot it? Sign in with a code". Once signed in, you can set a new password under Account, and then use it in the iPhone app as well.

If you are still signed in somewhere, you can also sign in on the new device with a code (see above) and reset later.

How do I delete my account?

In the iPhone app: Account › Delete account. On the web: Delete account at the bottom of the Account page. Accounts with a password confirm it first.

Your account is deleted and every device is signed out. Groups keep their history (the receipt photos you attached included), and your name stays as an unclaimed member, so other people's balances stay right. This can't be undone. The privacy policy has the details.

Where are receipt photos kept, and who sees them?

When you add or edit an expense in the web app, tap "Attach receipt" to take a photo or pick one, and it is kept with that expense; a photo you read with "Scan" is attached too, and you can tap × to take it off before you save. The photo is shrunk on your device first (at most 1600 pixels on its longest side), which also removes data such as where it was taken, and then stored in Cloudflare Workers KV. Everyone in the group can see it, and anyone in the group can remove it from the expense's details. The copy sent to be scanned is still read by Cloudflare Workers AI and dropped.

After the expense is deleted, its photo stays one more day so you can restore it, then it is deleted one to two days after the deletion; after a group is deleted, within a day. Deleting your account does not delete the receipt photos you uploaded: they belong to the group's entries.

How are leftover cents rounded?

Amounts are counted in each currency's smallest unit: 1 cent for US dollars, 1 yen for Japanese yen, 1 dollar for New Taiwan dollars. Split $10 three ways and the shares are $3.34, $3.33 and $3.33. Who gets the extra cent is decided by a fixed order that each entry works out for itself, not always the person who paid: saving the entry again never moves it, and over many entries the extras spread across the group.

An entry in another currency is converted to the group's currency as a whole first, then split. The shares of every entry always add up to exactly its total, so everyone's balances in a group always add up to zero.
